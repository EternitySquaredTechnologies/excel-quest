ABOUT EXCEL QUEST

    
Excel Quest is a free browser game for learning Excel formulas by doing them. You work on a small sales sheet for Brightside Electronics, one mission at a time, from your first SUM to a full PivotTable. Every mission tells you what the answer should look like, and if something goes wrong you get a plain English hint, not just an error.

    
What you will practise

    
    
      
  • Basics: SUM, AVERAGE, MIN, MAX, COUNT, COUNTBLANK and ROUND.
    • 
      
  • Text cleaning: TRIM, PROPER, UPPER, LEFT, RIGHT, MID, LEN, VALUE and joining text with &.
    • 
      
  • Decisions: IF, nested IF, IFS, AND and OR.
    • 
      
  • Counting and adding with rules: COUNTIF, SUMIF, AVERAGEIF, COUNTIFS, SUMIFS and SUMPRODUCT.
    • 
      
  • Lookups: VLOOKUP, IFERROR, INDEX and MATCH, XLOOKUP, and locking ranges with $.
    • 
      
  • Dates: date maths, TEXT, WEEKDAY and DATE.
    • 
      
  • Data cleaning: finding duplicates, wildcards and messy status values.
    • 
      
  • Modern Excel: FILTER, UNIQUE, SORT, TEXTJOIN and MAXIFS with spilled results.
    • 
      
  • PivotTables: rows, values, Sum, Count, Average and Max.
    • 
    

    
Who it is for

    
Students, beginners and anyone training for a business analyst or data job. Pick Excel 2016 or Excel 2021 / Microsoft 365 and the missions match your version. It works on phones, tablets and computers, needs no account, and your progress moves between devices with a save code.
